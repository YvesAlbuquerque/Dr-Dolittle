# Project origins and history

## 2026 — An open research question

**Dr. Dolittle was initiated by Yves Albuquerque in 2026** as an independent, curiosity-driven exploration of the evolution and diversity of animal communication.

The initial discussions moved from the idea of using AI to interpret animal vocalizations toward a broader comparative research program: identify general communication phenomena and capabilities first, compare broad animal groups next, and select species and datasets only when evidence justifies doing so.

The project was made public so that anyone can explore, critique, contribute to, and build upon the work. It is not a Polite Goblin product, and it was not founded as a commercial venture.
