# Mission and principles

Dr. Dolittle is an independent, open research initiative exploring how communication systems emerge, vary, and evolve across the animal kingdom.

Our aim is to contribute reusable knowledge to humanity. Anyone may study, critique, use, adapt, and extend the repository's MIT-licensed original work, including for commercial purposes, subject to that license. No personal acknowledgment of the founder is required beyond applicable license notices.

We value empirical evidence, methodological transparency, intellectual humility, reproducibility, animal welfare, and contributions from researchers and curious people alike.

We do not equate similarity with shared ancestry, signal clusters with words, or undocumented capabilities with absent capabilities.

Scientific discoveries belong to the researchers who establish them; the project history records its beginnings without claiming ownership of future contributions.
