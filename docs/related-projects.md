# Related research and initiatives

**Status:** Curated discovery index, initial survey dated 2026-11-23. This is not a systematic review, endorsement, affiliation, or claim of exhaustive coverage. Follow each initiative's own policies for data and code.

## Communication-centered initiatives

| Initiative | Focus | Relevance and distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Earth Species Project](https://earthspecies.org/what-we-do/publications/) | AI methods and research on non-human communication | Relevant computational methods and publications; Dr. Dolittle emphasizes cross-modal comparative classification before species-specific modeling |
| [Project CETI](https://www.projectceti.org/research/index) | Sperm-whale vocal communication, robotics, field observations, and ML | Deep species-focused case study and methodological inspiration |
| [Wild Dolphin Project](https://www.wilddolphinproject.org/) | Long-term dolphin behavior and communication field research | Example of contextual behavioral observations; verify access terms before data use |
| [iMak AI Lab — Animals Communication](https://imak.ai/projects/animals-communication) | Vocalizations across and within species using ML | A nearby conceptual research direction worth tracking |

## Methods, open tools, and collaborative research

| Initiative | Focus | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [BirdNET](https://birdnet.cornell.edu/about/) | Open bioacoustic detection and biodiversity monitoring | Useful tools, monitoring methods and negative-control examples; species detection is not translation |
| [ManyPrimates](https://manyprimates.github.io/) | Multi-site comparative primate cognition | Model for collaborative research, sampling bias awareness and comparative methodology |
| [Talk2Nature resource directory](https://metivity.github.io/talk2nature/research/resources/) | Curated animal-communication papers, datasets, tools and rights notes | Discovery aid, not independent verification or a license clearance |

## Suggested contribution format

For a new project, provide: official URL, maintaining organization, research question, modalities, animal groups, outputs/publications, open data/code status (verified or unknown), and how it differs from Dr. Dolittle. Avoid implying partnership.

**Gap to address:** current examples skew heavily toward vocal communication and AI. Expand to chemical signaling, touch, visual displays, substrate vibration, electrocommunication, and comparative behavioral ecology.
